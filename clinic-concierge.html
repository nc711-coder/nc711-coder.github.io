Clinic Concierge: AI receptionist

An AI receptionist for a physical therapy clinic. It answers patients from the clinic's own documents and books real appointments.

Try the live demo →Source code on GitHub

What it does

  • Answers questions from the clinic's own documents, with clickable sources
  • Books appointments from live availability, after reading the details back
  • Sends emergencies to 911 before the AI sees the message
  • Hands off to the front desk with a summary when a patient asks for a person
  • Logs bookings to Google Sheets and email automatically (n8n)

Built with

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL with pgvector, an open-weight LLM, n8n, Google Cloud Run.

The clinic is fictional; please don't enter real personal or medical information. The demo may take a few seconds to wake up on the first visit.
